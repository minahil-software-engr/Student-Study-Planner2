About Planner

Learn more about the Student Study Planner.

Student Study Planner is a simple web application designed to help students organize their academic tasks, manage their study activities, and keep track of daily learning progress in one place.

Students can create study tasks by entering a task title, selecting a subject, and choosing the current status of the task. This makes it easier to organize different subjects and keep important study activities under control.

Tasks can be edited or deleted whenever required. Students can also filter their tasks and view only pending or completed tasks, making it easier to focus on the work that still needs to be completed.

The application uses browser localStorage to save each student's tasks. This means that the tasks remain available even after refreshing the page, allowing students to continue their planning without losing their tasks.

The planner also provides useful study tips, paragraph counting, and a reading mode to make the application more useful and comfortable for students during their study sessions.
